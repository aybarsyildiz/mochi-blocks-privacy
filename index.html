Mochi Blocks — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Mochi Blocks is a puzzle game developed by NetNucleus. This policy explains what data is handled when you play. The short version: none.

What the game collects

Nothing. Mochi Blocks has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, and no analytics. Your scores, settings, and unfinished games are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

Children

Because the game collects no data at all, this applies equally to players of every age.

Future versions

If a future update ever changes what data is handled — for example by introducing advertising — this policy will be updated at this address first, with a new effective date, and any such feature will ask for the consents the law requires before it is active.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support contact on the Mochi Blocks App Store page.
