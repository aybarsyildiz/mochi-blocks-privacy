Mochi Blocks — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

Mochi Blocks is a puzzle game developed by NetNucleus. This policy explains what data is handled when you play, and by whom.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. Mochi Blocks has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your scores, settings, and unfinished games are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. None of it is transmitted to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Mochi Blocks shows ads served by Google AdMob (from version 1.1). To serve and measure ads, Google may process data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, where you have allowed it), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. This processing is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Purchasing the one-time Remove Ads upgrade removes all advertising from the game, and with it all ad-related data processing.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA/UK): on first launch you are asked for consent before any ad is shown. You can change your choice at any time via Privacy Choices in the game's settings panel.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): iOS asks separately for permission to track. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalized.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

Mochi Blocks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.

Contact
